Orbitext Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-18

Orbitext (the "App") is an independent, third-party client for the Telegram messaging platform, designed for Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the App processes, where it goes, and what choices you have.

Orbitext is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram FZ-LLC. Orbitext uses the Telegram API and is part of the Telegram ecosystem. The source code is available at github.com/vsiioky/orbitext.

1. Who we are

Orbitext is developed and maintained by an individual developer. Contact: @vldlb on Telegram.

2. Data the App processes

  • Telegram account data. To use the App you sign in with your Telegram account using your phone number. All communication with Telegram servers is performed by the official TDLib library bundled with the App. Your messages, chats, contacts, and media are handled by Telegram according to the Telegram Privacy Policy. We do not operate Telegram servers and we never receive a copy of your messages.
  • Local cache on your device. The App stores Telegram session data, downloaded media, and message previews in the App sandbox on your Apple Watch (and the paired iPhone where applicable). You can clear this cache in Settings > Storage > Clear Cache, or by deleting the App. The data never leaves your device.
  • Push notification token (APNs). If you enable notifications, Apple's push service provides a device token that is sent to Telegram so they can deliver message notifications. No content of the message leaves Telegram via this channel beyond what Telegram's own push system carries.
  • Diagnostic logs. The App writes local logs on your device for troubleshooting (which screen failed, which network call timed out). Logs stay on the device unless you explicitly share them with the developer when reporting an issue.

3. Data we do not collect

The App does not have its own analytics backend. It does not contain advertising SDKs, attribution SDKs, or third-party trackers. Your phone number is sent only to Telegram during sign-in, never to us.

Data obtained from Telegram is never used to train or develop AI or machine-learning models.

4. Third-party services

  • Telegram. All messaging functionality. See Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Push Notification service. Notification delivery. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Your rights

You can sign out at any time (Settings > Account > Log Out), which clears your Telegram session from the device. To delete your Telegram account entirely, use the Telegram service directly. To delete all locally cached App data, delete the App from your watch and the paired iPhone.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. The same age rules as Telegram itself apply.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this URL and the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be announced via the support channel.

8. Contact

Questions, problems, data requests: @vldlb on Telegram.
